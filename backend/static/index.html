Connect the Vexo band

The band's microphone records the lecture so Ketchup can catch you up.

  1. Charge it and turn it on. Use band Vexo-HACK-08.
  2. Close anything else connected to it, like the Vexo dashboard or a teammate's laptop. The band takes one connection at a time.
  3. Turn on Bluetooth on this Mac. If macOS asks whether Terminal can use Bluetooth, click Allow.
  4. Double-tap the band. Its light blinks blue for 60 seconds, which means it's ready.
  5. Click Connect now while it's blinking. When the light turns solid blue, the mic is on.
